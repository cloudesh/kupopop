Kupo Skribbl 0.11.0 polishes your art book and adds room word lists and suggestions.

- Drawing tools use small coloured stationery illustrations; Shapes shows several shapes. Like buttons no longer display a count.
- Host Room has a Custom Words tab for comma-separated words. They stay private to the room; custom-list games keep normal match scores but do not add lifetime rankings or likes.
- Word categories use clean expandable strips, Back actions have a compact arrow treatment, and dark paper uses quieter neutral colours.
- Suggestions on the home page accepts general feedback or a word/category for private maintainer review. Submissions are bounded and limited to five per day, one per minute; words are never added automatically.
- The server review adds command-name validation and dedicated snapshot/leaderboard request limits. Scores, choices, deadlines, likes and moderation remain server-authoritative. Coordinated answer sharing or multiple accounts cannot be reliably prevented without stronger identity checks.

Experimental custom-repository release. Native previews and protocol tests do not replace in-game verification.
