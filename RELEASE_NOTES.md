Kupo Skribbl 0.10.0 adds leaderboards and a little appreciation for your artists.

- A new Leaderboards tab ranks best single-game score, overall score, games played and drawing likes.
- Recorded lifetime stats appear anonymously with portraits by default. Include my name is a separate choice; Leaderboards and Profile settings both offer portrait hiding and whole-leaderboard opt-out. Turning off statistics removes your totals and likes. Existing statistics consent is preserved.
- Give another artist a thumbs-up once per drawing. The server rejects self-likes and stale turns, remembers likes across reconnects, and never adds them to match points.
- Job-icon portraits are removed. Existing job presets reset to the account's default moogle; custom drawing pixels are preserved. The seven moogle presets remain.

A recognisable portrait can identify its owner even under an Anonymous name. Hide it or opt out if you prefer. This remains an experimental custom-repository release; actual FFXIV rendering needs player verification.
