Kupo Skribbl 0.9.2 tidies the window and drawing turns.

- Uses the standard Dalamud window frame and title-bar dragging throughout. Custom paper drag zones and duplicate window controls are removed.
- Dark mode uses quieter plum and slate notes.
- Room leaders cannot be vote-kicked, including after leadership transfers. Vote skip remains available.
- New rooms offer six words by default.
- Cursor packets arriving just after a turn ends no longer show an error.
- New-profile setup offers a clearly explained action to enable lifetime stats, profile sharing and closed-window invitations together, plus a private setup alternative. Existing privacy choices are preserved; Profile settings has an Enable all three action.

This is an experimental custom-repository release. In-game dragging and rendering still require player verification.
