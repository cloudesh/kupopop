Kupo Skribbl 0.9.1 gives friends and profiles a fresh page.

- Friends now has separate Requests, Friends, Sent and Recently Played tabs, with clearer portrait cards and aligned actions.
- Invited games stand out with rose paper, a subtle gradient, and a direct Join invited game action.
- Dark mode uses a more consistent ink/slate palette. Portraits get a white backing in dark mode without changing their saved transparency.
- Shared profiles have a portrait header and separate lifetime-stat cards. Artist settings and privacy/invitation settings have their own pages.
- New-profile setup recommends sharing with friends and room partners, with a visible switch and explanation before account creation. Existing privacy choices are preserved. Statistics remain a separate opt-in.
- Optional closed-window invitations check once a minute over authenticated HTTPS. They do not maintain a game connection, join rooms, or open the plugin automatically. Accepted friends can queue a five-minute invitation for players who enabled the option; a Dalamud notification lets them open their book.

Enable background invitation checks under Profile → Privacy & invitations. This remains an experimental custom-repository release; in-game rendering and notification delivery require player verification.
