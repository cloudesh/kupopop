Kupo Skribbl 0.9.0 adds friends and makes connections follow your open book.

- The plugin starts closed. Closing the book leaves your room and disconnects; reopening starts outside your previous room.
- Centered the empty-lobby invitation and Host Room button.
- Display names are unique regardless of letter case or repeated spaces. Existing duplicates receive a short suffix without losing their accounts.
- Right-click room artists to add friends or view profiles. Accept requests and manage friends and recent session partners in the new Friends tab.
- Hosts can invite online friends. Invitations stay inside the plugin, expire after five minutes, and can be accepted from Friends or Play.
- Profile sharing and lifetime statistics are optional and start disabled. Opt in from Profile to record completed games, wins, total points, and best match; reset or disable them at any time. These points are statistics, not currency.
- Private operator monitoring now shows live room, connection, and game counts without names, room codes, messages, or answers.

Protocol 5 requires this plugin update. Existing profiles, avatars, recovery codes, and current match scoring are preserved. This remains an experimental custom-repository release; rendering inside FFXIV still needs user verification.
